About the Author

Saurabh Kumar Dey is a Founder, Entrepreneur & Author working across technology, digital identity, IoT hardware, writing and personal exploration.

He is the founder of SmartSOS and IdentiQ, and the author of The Third Row: A Memoir.

His work spans physical technology, digital identity and literary non-fiction, bringing together his interests in building practical systems, exploring difficult questions and documenting personal experiences through writing.

Work & Projects

SmartSOS

SmartSOS is an IoT accident detection and emergency response system designed to detect significant impacts and automatically initiate emergency alerts.

The system combines hardware and connected technologies including an ESP32, MPU6050, GSM and GPS components.

Explore SmartSOS

IdentiQ

IdentiQ is a digital identity platform designed to help professionals and businesses create, manage and share their professional identity.

The platform combines digital profiles, NFC and QR sharing, portfolios, contact sharing, lead capture, analytics, wallet passes and other tools for managing a professional digital presence.

Writing

Writing is an important part of Saurabh Kumar Dey's work, particularly literary non-fiction, memoir and philosophical exploration.

His writing extends beyond his professional work and explores questions surrounding identity, ambition, relationships, social status, personal experience and the process of understanding oneself.

The Third Row: A Memoir

The Third Row: A Memoir is the debut book by Saurabh Kumar Dey.

The memoir explores social status, ambition, lost dreams, relationships, family, sacrifice, identity and the search for self-worth beyond external titles and expectations.

Drawing from the author's experiences and roots in Banaras, the book examines how childhood experiences, institutional hierarchies, relationships and personal ambitions can shape the way a person understands their own identity.

At its core, the memoir asks what remains of a person when the titles, relationships, ambitions and roles through which they defined themselves begin to disappear.

Background

Saurabh Kumar Dey was born in Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh and is currently based in Pune, Maharashtra.

His background and experiences in Varanasi, commonly known as Banaras, form an important part of the personal and cultural context explored in The Third Row.

His professional journey combines entrepreneurship, technology development, hardware experimentation, digital identity and writing.

Ideas & Philosophy

Saurabh's work is guided by a simple builder-oriented philosophy: ideas matter when they are turned into something tangible.

His broader creative work also focuses on asking questions that are difficult to answer and exploring what those questions reveal about people, technology and life.

This approach appears across his technology projects, philosophical writing and literary work.

Other Writing

In addition to The Third Row, Saurabh Kumar Dey writes about technology, ideas, philosophy, building products and personal experiences.

His broader writing and essays explore questions that sit outside conventional technology and entrepreneurship narratives.
